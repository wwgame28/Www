# /brag — Рикошет

**What:** arcade shooter for iPhone, in the browser — you get one projectile and must pick it back up (or catch it mid-air for ×2).
**For:** the owner and friends, competing on high scores and the daily challenge.
**Different:** one bullet. Every shot is a decision; the weapon *is* its trajectory.
**Most impressive claim:** 7 heroes = 7 trajectories, 7 locations, a daily run that friends replay exactly.
**Visual hook:** a gold projectile ricocheting around the frame under "ОДНА ПУЛЯ."
**Real UI shown:** menu, third-person gameplay (bot-played real build, frame-exact capture), heroes screen, four locations.
**Tone:** default — punchy, playful, gold-on-ink like the game's own UI.
**Share line:** Одна пуля. Верни её — или конец.
