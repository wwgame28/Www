## Controls & camera

The controls work, but the default third-person view is set up like a landscape shooter. On a portrait iPhone the camera sees only 31° horizontally (39° in Safari). It spends 35% of the screen on sky, pins the hero at x≈108 of 390 px, and only ±0.8 m beside the hero is visible, so most threats and the dropped projectile are off-screen. Aiming is manual yaw only, with no assist and a fixed 8 m target, so the spiral and black hole can't choose their range. The fire button grabs any touch within 102 px and has no cancel, so accidental throws are likely, and in a one-projectile game that is the most costly input error. The joystick has a fixed origin, no dead zone and a 102 px grab circle (in top-down a missed grab even throws). One-handed play, which the spec lists as a success criterion, is not possible in third person. Most fixes are numbers in render.js placeCamera/setView and input.js, plus a soft lock in main.js; no GitHub controller fits the no-build, pure-simulation setup. All figures are computed in node with the vendored three r169 and the real sim; nothing was measured on an iPhone.

### Рекомендации

**1. Re-frame the third-person camera for portrait and make the orbit rigid** — влияние 5/5, объём S

*Зачем:* Current rig (5.4 m back, 3.4 m up, 0.75 m right shoulder, vfov 62°, pitch 14°): horizontal FOV is 31° (39° in Safari). 35% of the screen is sky. The hero is pinned in the left quarter (feet at x≈108/390), and only ±0.8 m beside the hero is visible: a skeleton 1 m to the left is off-screen. Turning also feels loose: the position lerps while lookAt snaps to the new yaw, so the crosshair slides 11/27/45 px during 300/800/1500 px/s drags, and on fast flicks the camera dives from 5.45 m to 4.25 m (22% closer).

*Как:* render.js placeCamera (tps): stop lerping camera.position. Keep a smoothed pivot instead: pivot.lerp(heroPos, 1-exp(-10*dt)); position = pivot - f*4.6 + r*0.3 at y 5.6; lookAt(pivot + f*3.5 + r*0.3, 0.4, ...) → pitch ≈33°; apply yaw unsmoothed (rigid orbit, crosshair no longer drifts). setView/resize: camera.fov = clamp(2*atan(tan(21°)/aspect), 62°, 72°). Computed at 390×844 / 390×664: sky 35%→15%/11%; side view at the hero ±0.8→±1.9 m / ±1.3→±2.2 m; 3 m danger circle visible 66→82% / 77→86%; hero still 81/71 px tall (was 142/112); crosshair moves from 48% to ~36% of screen height, away from the thumbs. fadeOccluders: make the test height-aware. Store each occluder's Box3 max.y in setLocation and fade only if the camera→hero-chest (y 0.9) line passes below o.h+0.2 at the closest point; otherwise the higher camera will fade knee-high rocks, graves and stumps. Also add tall edge decor (city streetlight ≈2.5 m, trafficlight_A ≈2.9 m) to occluders. Set material.transparent only while fade<0.99. Cemetery: fogNear 6→8 so the hero (now ~7.3 m from the camera) stays sharp.

**2. Soft-lock aim assist for the single throw (both views)** — влияние 5/5, объём M

*Зачем:* Every miss costs a 1.4–1.9 s run to fetch the projectile (simulated). Manual third-person aim must be within ±5° at 8 m (hit radius 0.4+0.3 m), which is ±13 px of thumb accuracy at 0.372°/px. Spiral and black hole always go off ~8 m ahead (AIM_DIST), so the Stone Mage, a starter hero, can't choose distance at all. In top-down, skeletons are only ~18 px wide (22 px/m).

*Как:* main.js: replace aheadOfHero() with aimPoint(). Candidates are alive enemies 1–16 m away, within ±10° of yaw (±16° under 4 m), on screen (view.worldToScreen().inside), with a clear line (segment vs game.solids circles). Score = angle/cone + 0.35*d/16 and take the lowest. Keep the previous lock until it leaves 1.4× the cone (hysteresis, no flicker). Target = that enemy's x/z, so spiral and black hole trigger on it; with no lock, fall back to 8 m straight. Use aimPoint() in the fire path, in previewPath (the aim line then shows the assisted path) and in placeCrosshair: snap the crosshair to the enemy at y 0.8 with a .lock style. Optional magnetism: ×0.6 look gain while a slow drag is over the locked enemy. Top-down: if the release point is within 1.2 m of an enemy, aim at its center. render.js sync: while shootT>0 in tps, face g.hero.facing (the real throw direction) instead of yaw+π. Settings toggle «Автоприцел», on by default.

**3. Show nearby threats and the dropped projectile at the hero's feet; stop point-blank wall spawns** — влияние 4/5, объём S

*Зачем:* Even after the camera fix, portrait third person shows only ~±2 m beside the hero and ~2.5 m behind. 60% of spawns (left/right/bottom) come from off the starting view axis, and deaths from skeletons you can't see feel unfair. The projectile's off-screen arrow lands under the thumbs: a projectile 7 m behind gives an arrow at (110, 808) on iPhone 14, right under the joystick. At the arena edges, walkers rise 0.9 m inside the wall, so a hero hugging the wall can get one within 0.5–1 m that touches him almost as soon as its 0.9 s rise ends.

*Как:* render.js: add 8 'danger arc' sectors to the hero group (RingGeometry 0.62–0.80 m, thetaLength π/4, coral MeshBasicMaterial, depthWrite false). Each frame set sector opacity = max over alive enemies in that sector of (1 − d/7); include rising enemies and pulse while e.rise>0. On the same ring, a gold chevron at 0.95 m points to the projectile while it is dropped. main.js updateHud: clamp the edge arrow to y ≤ innerHeight − 260 px so it never sits under the stick or fire button. Cache the canvas rect in render.resize() instead of calling getBoundingClientRect in every worldToScreen (right now that forces layouts after the HUD writes every frame). game.js spawnPos: if a spawn falls within 3.5 m of the hero, mirror `along` to −along (still deterministic for a given input stream).

**4. Make the fire button forgiving: smaller grab zone, drag-down cancel, edge-turn, look acceleration, fire buffer** — влияние 4/5, объём M

*Зачем:* With one projectile, an accidental throw is the worst input error. Right now any touch within 102 px of the fire button center (STICK_PX×ZONE, 1.8× its 56 px radius) becomes a fire hold, so a look-drag started near the button throws on release, and there is no cancel. Hold-and-drag is the aim gesture, but the button center is 84 px from the right edge: you can turn only 28° right (110° left) before you have to lift, which throws. A 180° turn needs 483 px of drag at LOOK_SENS 0.0065.

*Как:* input.js: use separate radii, FIRE_GRAB = 70 px (visual 56 + 14) and 102 px only for the stick. While fire is held, if clientY − y0 > 80 px set fire.cancel: the button shows ✕ and main.js greys the aim line; releasing while cancelled throws nothing, and moving back above 80 px re-arms. Edge-turn: while a look or fire pointer is within 28 px of the left/right screen edge, add ±2.5 rad/s of yaw. Look acceleration: track per-pointer speed v (px/s, ~50 ms EMA); gain = 0.0050 rad/px at v≤400, rising to 0.0110 at v≥1600 (smoothstep). Slow drags get 0.29°/px (finer than today's 0.37°) and a 280 px flick turns ≈170°; takeLook() returns radians. main.js: if fire is released while the projectile isn't in hand, keep the request for 0.20 s and fire it on pickup, catch or boomerang return (snappy catch-and-throw ×2). Hint: add «потяни вниз — отмена».

**5. Joystick: semi-floating origin, dead zone, faster response, bigger grab zone** — влияние 4/5, объём S

*Зачем:* The origin is fixed at the ring center, with no dead zone and linear speed up to 60 px. A thumb that lands 40 px off-center instantly runs at 67% speed in a direction you didn't choose. 2–3 px of resting-thumb jitter slides the hero in the Idle pose, and reversing takes a 120 px thumb trip. In top-down, a left-thumb touch more than 102 px from the center becomes a free touch and throws on release. After a perk pick or resume, setEnabled(false) wipes the held stick, so the hero stays frozen until you lift and press again.

*Как:* input.js: grab the stick for any touch with x < 50% of the width (mirrored for stick-right) and y > 55% of the height, excluding the fire grab circle. On grab, set x0/y0 to the touch point (clamped ≥70 px from the screen edges) and move #stick there with a CSS transform; it springs back home on release (120 ms). Magnitude m = clamp((l − 8)/(42 − 8), 0, 1): 8 px dead zone, full speed at 42 px instead of 60; the knob is still drawn out to 60 px. Follow: if l > 60, drag x0/y0 along so l stays 60, which makes reversing instant. setEnabled(false) should clear only fire/fires/lookDx and make move() return 0, so a held stick keeps working after a perk pick or resume. Reference behavior: nipplejs 'semi' + 'follow' modes.

**6. A real one-handed mode (the spec's success criterion) and a clear left-handed label** — влияние 4/5, объём M

*Зачем:* The spec's success goal is 'playable one-handed', but the default third person needs two thumbs: the stick (bottom-left) and fire button (bottom-right) are 214 px apart, and turning needs another drag. Today only top-down is one-thumb playable, and only by switching between steering and tapping. The existing 'Джойстик справа' toggle already mirrors the stick, fire button and ammo chip correctly (input.js reads the element rects).

*Как:* Add a setting «Одной рукой» (body.onehand). The stick sits in the thumb's corner, with the fire button 150 px directly above it on the same side. A tap anywhere on the 3D view (≤200 ms, ≤12 px movement) throws at view.screenToGround(tap), snapped to the nearest enemy within 60 px on screen. The camera auto-turns toward the run direction (≤100°/s × stick magnitude), but only while the stick's forward component is > −0.3, so backpedalling keeps your aim. In this mode the hero faces the movement direction (render.sync face = g.hero.facing), and the fire button uses the soft lock along that facing. Rename the existing toggle to «Левша (джойстик справа)» and mirror the new stick zone and edge-turn with it.

**7. Faster pickup: short base vacuum plus a beacon** — влияние 3/5, объём S

*Зачем:* 400 random throws per weapon on the forest layout: the projectile drops a median 9.4 m (ricochet), 8.9 m (snake), 8.7 m (lightning) or 8.0 m (black hole) from the thrower, which is 1.5–1.9 s of running; only the boomerang always returns. In third person the last metre is the fiddly part, because the projectile is usually beside or behind the view and the pickup radius is 0.85 m centre to centre (hero radius 0.4).

*Как:* game.js: raise the base pickup radius from 0.85 to 1.1 m (×reach). Add a base vacuum: while dropped and dHero < 1.8 m, move the projectile toward the hero at 8 m/s (it hops into the hand; 'pickup' is emitted as now). Start the magnet perk beyond that (2.5 + 2×lvl m) so it stays valuable. render.js: while dropped, show a vertical beam over the projectile (open CylinderGeometry r 0.12 m, h 3 m, additive, opacity 0.35, depthWrite false) so it is visible over obstacles and through cemetery fog. Combined with the gold chevron on the hero ring and the 0.20 s fire buffer, running over the projectile and throwing again becomes one motion.

**8. GitHub check: no ready-made third-person controller fits; borrow its numbers instead** — влияние 2/5, объём S

*Зачем:* The owner asked whether a ready-made third-person control with animations exists on GitHub. The closest is pmndrs/viverse (MIT; works with vanilla three.js: SimpleCharacter + ScreenJoystick + camera collision + animations). But it is TypeScript that needs npm and a bundler, pulls in three-mesh-bvh, @pixiv/three-vrm, @pmndrs/timeline and @viverse/sdk, and brings its own BVH physics and character/animation pipeline. Adopting it would replace the deterministic pure sim in game.js that daily seeds, challenge links and the tests rely on, as well as the KayKit clip mapping. The others don't fit either: pmndrs/ecctrl needs React Three Fiber + Rapier; yomotsu/camera-controls (MIT) is an orbit camera whose own pointer handling would fight input.js; SimonDev's ThirdPersonCamera (MIT) has no touch input; nipplejs (MIT) is only a joystick.

*Как:* Keep the in-house input.js plus the render.js camera rig (~110 lines) and copy the proven values. From nipplejs: 'semi' mode (catchDistance) and 'follow' for the stick. From viverse: drag-look speed of 4 rad per screen height (0.0047 rad/px at 844 px; the current 0.0065 is in range), a 0.2 m camera collision offset, and a 10 rad/s turn cap for the character's yaw. From camera-controls: treat draggingSmoothTime 0.125 s as the upper bound for any follow smoothing. For more animations, the original KayKit Adventurers pack (76 clips) already has 1H_Ranged_Aiming, 2H_Ranged_Aiming and Spellcast_Raise for a hold-to-aim pose, and Walking_A for low stick deflection; re-export those rather than importing a controller.

## GitHub controllers

GitHub does have ready third-person controllers with animations, but none fits this repo as a drop-in. The only active plain-three.js one is hh-hang/three-player-controller (MIT, 300 stars). It ships 308 KB of ESM (73 KB gzipped), needs three-mesh-bvh and `three/examples/jsm/...` imports, runs the hero's movement through its own capsule physics (game.js must own that), and uses a two-thumb layout: left stick plus a right-half look pad. ecctrl needs React 19, R3F and Rapier and three r184 or later. @pmndrs/viverse is 2.7 MB with VRM and timeline dependencies. Sketchbook is archived, on three 0.113, and has no touch input. src/input.js, placeCamera and fadeOccluders already do what matters on a phone, so the verdict is to keep our code and copy three techniques: upper/lower-body animation masks (viverse), phase-synced crossfades (SimonDev and the three r169 examples), and a stick dead zone plus fading of edge decor. While testing these on the KayKit rig in node with the vendored three r169, I reproduced two real bugs. First, firing while moving leaves the hero frozen in the rest pose (T-pose) until the movement clip changes. Second, the hand-prop filter never runs, so every hero holds every prop of its class. I also measured that the Throw clip releases at 0.72 s, but the shoot window is 0.45 s and only plays when standing still, so 4 of the 7 heroes never visibly throw. No GitHub controller handles one-thumb portrait play, so that is still our own design work.

### Рекомендации

**1. Fix: hero freezes in rest pose (T-pose) after firing while moving** — влияние 5/5, объём S

*Зачем:* Firing while running is the most common action in the game. Right now the hero turns into a rest-pose (T-pose) mannequin and slides around until the player stops or changes direction. That looks broken on the first run.

*Как:* src/render.js: effect('shoot') (line 297) sets hero.current = null. On the next frame sync() asks again for the clip that is already playing (Running_A, a strafe or Walking_Backwards). animate() (lines 100-116) then calls next.reset().fadeIn() and v.action.fadeOut() on the SAME action, because the fade>0 branch has no `v.action !== next` guard. The weight fades 1->0 and three.js disables the action, which leaves the rest pose. Fix: in animate(), right after `const next = v.mixer.clipAction(clip);` add `if (next === v.action) { if (ONE_SHOT.has(clipName)) next.reset().setEffectiveWeight(1).play(); v.current = clipName; return; }`. Checked in node with vendored r169 and Barbarian.glb. Old code: 0.36 s after the shot the arm bone is 0.0 deg from the rest pose, no actions are enabled, and it stays that way at 2.4 s. Patched: Running_A stays at weight 1.00, and a second Throw restarts cleanly. Regression test: a node --test loader hook that maps 'three' to vendor/three.module.min.js and shims globalThis.ProgressEvent. With textures stripped, the vendored GLTFLoader parses the GLBs in node; scratchpad/glbcheck/fix.mjs is a working template.

**2. Upper-body shoot layer (viverse mask technique) and Throw timing** — влияние 5/5, объём M

*Зачем:* Today the throw animation only plays when the hero stands still (sync() checks `else if (shootT > 0)` after the moving branch). Even then, the 4 Throw heroes stop before the arm swings: release is at 0.72 s, the window is 0.45 s. So the core action, throwing your one projectile, has almost no visible animation. With a separate arm layer the hero can run and throw at the same time.

*Как:* Copy pmndrs/viverse packages/viverse/src/animation/mask.ts (`clip.tracks.filter(...)`, MIT). In setHero, build two clip sets that share one mixer. Track names are GLTFLoader-sanitized (dots removed). `const UPPER=/^(spine|chest|head|upperarm[lr]|lowerarm[lr]|wrist[lr]|hand[lr]|handslot[lr]|elbowIK[lr]|handIK[lr])\./; const part=(up)=>h.animations.map(c=>new THREE.AnimationClip(c.name,c.duration,c.tracks.filter(t=>UPPER.test(t.name)===up)));` then `hero={...,mixer,legs:{mixer,animations:part(false)},arms:{mixer,animations:part(true)}}`. For Barbarian this splits 48 upper / 72 lower tracks, so the mixer does the same work as now. In sync(): `animate(hero.legs, clip, 0.12)`. Then `animate(hero.arms, shootT>0 ? hero.shootClip : clip, 0.1)`. When the arms go back to a locomotion clip, set `hero.arms.action.time = hero.legs.action.time` so they stay in step. In effect('shoot'), reset only hero.arms.current, then set `hero.arms.action.time = 0.5` for 'Throw' with shootT about 0.5. That puts the swing 0.1-0.25 s after the tap. Death_A, Idle and clearRun drive both layers. Hit_A on 'hurt' then works on the arms only; Hit_A, PickUp and Cheer are listed in ONE_SHOT but never played today. Prototype in node: during run+throw the arm pose equals the pure Throw pose (0.0 deg) and the legs equal pure Running_A (0.0 deg). Measured release (peak hand speed): Throw 0.72 s of 1.37 s, the same on all heroes; Spellcast_Shoot 0.08 s, so the mages are fine.

**3. Fix: every hero holds all of its class props** — влияние 4/5, объём S

*Зачем:* The hand-prop filter in setHero never runs. The Knight shows a 1H and a 2H sword, an offhand sword and 4 shields. The Barbarian shows 2 axes, a mug, an offhand axe and a shield. The Rogue shows 2 crossbows, a knife, a throwable and an offhand knife. The heroes look cluttered and the weapon doesn't read.

*Как:* src/render.js setHero, lines 223-226: `h.inner.getObjectByName(side)` with 'handslot.l'/'handslot.r' returns undefined. GLTFLoader r169 (createUniqueName -> PropertyBinding.sanitizeNodeName) strips '.' from node names, so the bones are 'handslotl'/'handslotr'. Change to `h.inner.getObjectByName(THREE.PropertyBinding.sanitizeNodeName(side))`. Checked in node with the vendored loader: 'handslot.l' gives undefined, 'handslotl' gives children 1H_Axe_Offhand and Barbarian_Round_Shield. The same naming rule applies to any bone-name regex, including the mask above.

**4. Keep our controller and camera: adopt none of the GitHub controllers** — влияние 4/5, объём S

*Зачем:* A rewrite around any of them would lose the deterministic sim (daily seed, challenge links, tests), the fire-button aim and the portrait layout, and would add a large dependency to a phone game. None of them improves what the player feels.

*Как:* Verdicts and reasons. ecctrl 2.0.2 (MIT, 805 stars) needs React >=19.2.7, R3F >=9.4, drei, Rapier >=2.2, leva and three >=0.184: impossible here. three-player-controller 0.6.2 (MIT, active; peer three >=0.159 accepts our r169): dist/index.mjs is 308,593 bytes, plus three-mesh-bvh.
